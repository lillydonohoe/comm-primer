## AI Has a Say, Too
 
When people use AI, it is often seen as a tool to help with writing. However, AI does more than just copy what someone has written. AI chooses certain words and phrases based on patterns learned from stored data. These choices that AI makes independently alter the final message you are using to communicate.

An example of this is if you were to ask AI to create an apology that was formal. AI may remove any humor or emotion that you originally included and instead make it more formal and serious. This may not express the feelings you wanted to express originally. Even though AI does not have human intentions or emotions, it can still alter your original message.

The sender of the message has a responsibility to make sure the content AI created is accurate, appropriate, and consistent with what they intended to communicate. AI participates in creating your message, but you need to remain responsible for what you ultimately send.
