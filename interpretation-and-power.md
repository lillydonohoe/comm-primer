## What You Mean Vs. What They Hear

You can carefully chose your words and still be misubderstood. People do not inturpret messages based on just words alone. They rely on the relationship with the other person, expectations, and understanding of the situation. When AI is involved this can get even mroe complicated.

If you were to ask AI to message your coworker and make the message sound confident, the final version might sound professional to you but your coworker might recieve it as demanding or arrogant. Your intention was to communicate confidently, hwoever the recipient can misinterpret it as soemthing else.

A short AI response can seem polite to the sender, but cold to a close friend who expexted a more personal message. There is never a guarentee that someone will view the message exaclty as you mean it. So, when AI is involved, this creates more room for misinterpretation.

**How can you avoid misunderstanding?**
- Think about who is recieving your message and your relationship with them.
- Read the message from teh recipients perspective.
- Ask whether the tone matches what you orignially meant to communciate.

**Remember:**You always control what you send. You can take advice and suggestions from AI, but in the end, good communciaiton is when you consider both. </p>
