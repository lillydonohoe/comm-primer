## What You Mean Vs. What They Hear

You can carefully choose your words and still be misunderstood. People do not interpret messages based on words alone. They rely on the relationship with the other person, expectations, and understanding of the situation. When AI is involved, this can get even more complicated.

If you were to ask AI to message your coworker and make the message sound confident, the final version might sound professional to you, but your coworker might receive it as demanding or arrogant. Your intention was to communicate confidently, the recipient can misinterpret it as something else.

A short AI response can seem polite to the sender, but cold to a close friend who expected a more personal message. There is never a guarantee that someone will view the message exactly as you mean it. So, when AI is involved, this creates more room for misinterpretation.

**How can you avoid misunderstanding?**
- Think about who is receiving your message and your relationship with them.
- Read the message from the recipient's perspective.
- Ask whether the tone matches what you originally meant to communicate.

**Remember:**You always control what you send. You can take advice and suggestions from AI, but in the end, good communication is when you consider both.
