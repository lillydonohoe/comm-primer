## Words Have Meaning

Have you ever been sent a message that seemed rude, even though you did not think the person sending it was trying to be rude? There are many small changes one can make in a message to completely change the meaning of the message. When you hear "sure" versus "Absolutely!," the message seems very different, although they have the same intention. They both share the intention of agreement, but they create a very different impression depending on the situation. When AI tries to make a text more professional or friendly, you see these small changes. This ultimately changes the whole tone of the message you wrote originally.
  
**What should you take away from this?** 
 
When AI changes your message, even if it is just your wording, it could change the social meaning of your message. Before sending an AI-edited message, it is important to reread it and be certain that it is the tone you want to send. Ask yourself if these new words communicate how you feel and if your intentions are still shared in the message. The best wording is what fits the person and situation you are communicating too.
