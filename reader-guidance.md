## Think Before You Send 

AI is a very useful tool to assist in communication; however, the final message should be a reflection of your own personal intentions. When the sender trusts AI to help edit or write a message, that sender is still responsible for taking a moment to review it. 

**Before You Send Checklist**
1. Does this message sound like me? 
  Look for words that you would normally use, and change anything that does not feel natural to you.
2. Does it communicate what I intended? 
  Compare your original message to the version AI gave you. Make sure AI did not change any major points or emotions. 
3. How may the other person interpret this message? 
  You need to be aware of your audience. In personal communication, you should focus on keeping your relationships and emotions clear in the message. In professional communication, your message should more accurately follow workplace expectations. Make sure your wording fits the situation you are communicating in. 
4. Is everything accurate?
  Check for spelling in names, facts, citations, and other small details that matter. 

**Use AI Without Losing Your Voice**
You do not have to stop using AI in general, just be aware of how it can affect the way you communicate. Be aware that you should be using it intentionally. 

AI should correct grammar, organize your own thoughts, or suggest where you could add clearer wording. Having AI automatically rewrite your message for you is where your voice can get lost. 

Before you send, make sure you mean it.
