## How AI Changes Your Message

**Communication may seem simple:** One person sends a message, and another person receives it. However, when AI is involved in the process, it becomes more complicated.

The message no longer goes directly from the sender to the receiver, it passes through AI first. This is when someone writes what they intend to say, and AI rewrites it, meaning the recipient is now receiving the edited version. AI can correct spelling, organize thoughts, and make it easier to understand. There are benefits that come with using AI. However, it also introduces problems when it is changing the original message that the sender might not have intended to send.

An example of this is someone writing, "I am upset that you canceled again." If AI were to rewrite this, it may edit it as, "I am disappointed by your repeated cancellations." This makes the message more formal and confrontational. The words may be clearer; however, the edits AI made may have added more emotion.

 **Why does this matter?** AI can help improve how you deliver your words, but it can not fully understand the history, relationship, or emotions behind the message. Remember that clear communication is more than having no spelling and grammatical errors; every message also is a reflection of what you want to say and how you feel.
