  <h2 id="transmission-title">How AI Changes Your Message</h2>
  <p class="lede">Communication may seem simple: One person sends a message, and another person recieves it. However, when AI is involved in the process it becomes more complicated.</p>The message no longer goes directly from the sender to the reciever, it passes through AI first. This is when someone writes what they intend to say, and AI rewrites it, meaning the recipient is now recieveing the edited version.</p>AI can correct spelling, organize thoughts, and make it easier to understand. There are benefits that come with using AI. However, it also introduces problems when it is changing the original message th sender might not ahve intended to send.</p>An example of this is someone writing, "I am upset that you canceled again." If AI were to rewrite this it may edit it as, "I am disappointed by your reeated cancellations." This makes the message more formal and confrontational. The words may be clearer, however, the edits AI made may have added more emotions.</p> __Why does this matter?__</p>AI can help imporve how you deliver your words, but it can not fully understand the history, relationship, or emotions behind the message. Remember that clear communication is more than having no spelling and gramatical errors, every message aslso is a rflection of what you want to say and how you feel.</p>
